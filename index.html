    Timeline

      The map is schematic. Key addresses are geocoded from their 1963 street addresses, but streets are simplified and the Rebel Drive-In position is approximate. Croy and Westbrook gave no clock times in testimony, so their times are estimated from the order of events they described. Speeds are straight-line averages between documented points, so real speeds were somewhat higher; on dashed stretches they are minimums. Times come from Dallas police radio logs (accurate to a minute or two), Warren Commission reconstructions and witness estimates, which often disagree. Items marked “disputed” rest on contested testimony.
